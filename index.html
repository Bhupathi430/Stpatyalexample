Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Stpatyal Social Media Services ("Stpatyal", "we", "our"), protecting the privacy, identity, and sensitive commercial data of our clients is fundamental to our practice. When you seek assistance regarding compromised, locked, or disabled accounts, we operate under rigorous security safeguards.

2. Explicit Zero-Password Guarantee

We do not collect, request, record, or store passwords, two-factor authentication (2FA) SMS codes, or account backup keys under any circumstances. All legitimate platform recovery procedures and appeal dossiers are submitted through external channels that do not necessitate sharing sensitive credentials with our team.

3. Information We Collect

In order to evaluate your inquiry and prepare escalation filings, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, direct phone number.
  • Account details: Target social media handle (@username), profile URL, account creation timeframe.
  • Case documentation: Screenshots of system disablement notices, suspension emails, or impersonator profile links.
  • Government ID or verification documents (only when officially mandated by platform verification processes, handled with strict transient transmission).

4. Use of Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Diagnosing the cause of platform actions and determining case viability.
  • Formulating and submitting appeals, impersonation takedown dossiers, or copyright notices to platform review teams.
  • Direct communication regarding the progress of your case.

5. Anonymization in Case Demonstrations

Any case studies, forensic walkthroughs, or case summaries displayed on our portfolio are subjected to full anonymization. Personal usernames, private messages, customer faces, and proprietary identifiers are blurred or omitted in full compliance with non-disclosure standards.

6. Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, rent, or trade client information. Information is disclosed solely to authorized platform trust and safety escalation channels as required to execute your appeal with your explicit consent.

7. Data Deletion Inquiries

Clients hold the right to request the immediate purging of all case notes and evidence files following the conclusion of their case. For inquiries, contact: privacy@stpatyal.com.
